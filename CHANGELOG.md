## 0.1.0 - 2026-09-17

### Added
- Initial release of `grounded-hypercalculi`: Because there are more calculi than just maths'.
- Language Calculus: Formal syntax trees, alphabets, closed and non-well-founded quotation graphs, and Metamath proof bridging.
- Meta-Calculus: Symbolic dynamics of rewrite systems, trajectory spaces, shift operators, trajectory bisimulation, and path homotopy.
- Hyper-Calculus: Automorphism groups of semantic state spaces, permutation groups, Lie derivation algebras with Leibniz product rule and Lie commutator brackets.
- Ordinal Calculus: Left subtraction, discrete difference operators, normal functions, ordinal derivatives enumerating fixed points, and Veblen hierarchies.
- Real Analysis Calculus: Continuum constructions from grounded rationals, Dedekind cuts, Cauchy sequences, and Conway Surreal numbers with infinitesimals.
- Oracle Calculus: Turing jumps, oracle towers, leapfrog trans-omega computations ($O(\omega^\omega)$ in unit time), hyperoracles, and conclusion analysis.
